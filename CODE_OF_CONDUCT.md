# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing experience and backgrounds. Discuss evidence and tradeoffs, accept good-faith corrections, and keep reports reproducible.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content, personal attacks, publication of private information, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Critique the proposal, not the person. Do not share credentials, private conversations, or unsolicited advertising in project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed channels. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Contact the repository owner through an established private channel. If none is available, ask for one without publishing personal details or allegations. Maintainers should keep reports confidential to the extent practical and avoid requiring public disclosure of evidence.

Maintainers may request corrections, remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or remove access according to severity and repetition. They should explain the relevant standard and provide a private route to contest a decision. Maintainers must also follow this policy.

This policy does not replace the platform's safety, abuse-reporting, or legal processes.
